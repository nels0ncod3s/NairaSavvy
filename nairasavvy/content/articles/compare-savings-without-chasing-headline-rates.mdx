## Start with the job this money must do
Write down when you need access and whether you can tolerate a change in value. A balance you need for tomorrow’s expenses has a different purpose from money set aside for a distant goal.

## Put the terms next to the rate
For each product, record the currency, minimum balance, withdrawal restrictions, fees and whether the quoted rate can change. Leave unknown fields marked unknown. Ask the provider for the terms rather than filling gaps with assumptions.

## Compare like with like
A dollar yield and a naira yield cannot be ranked by percentage alone. Within the same currency, use the same time period and interest basis. Our [comparison table](/savings) separates currencies and shows what still needs verification.

## Understand the calculation
For a hypothetical annual return of 10% and inflation of 20%, the annual real return is (1.10 ÷ 1.20) − 1, or about −8.33%. The nominal balance grows while purchasing power falls. This example assumes constant rates and ignores fees and taxes.
