## Start with your usage
Check the ordinary data you used during your last recharge period. Note whether the plan expired before you finished it, or ran out before its expiry date.

## Set three filters
Enter your budget, the minimum validity you need and your ordinary-data requirement in the [data-plan comparison](/cut-costs/data-plans).

## Keep bonuses separate
Night data and app-specific data may not cover the work you need to do. Count a bonus only when you know its restrictions and expect to use it.

## Work out the unit value
For a hypothetical 2 GB plan costing ₦1,000, ordinary-data value is 2 GB per ₦1,000. A 3 GB plan at the same price has a higher unit value, but a shorter expiry or eligibility restriction could make it less useful to you.

## Check before purchasing
Confirm the current price, validity and eligibility in your provider’s official channel. Historical records in our tool are examples awaiting verification; their activation instructions may no longer work.
