## Write down your goal
Name the expense, its currency and the date when you will need the money. Keep that note beside any offer you are considering.

## Ask five questions
1. What exactly am I buying or depositing into?
2. Can the balance or return change?
3. When and how can I withdraw?
4. What fees, deductions or penalties apply?
5. Where can I independently verify the provider and the terms?

## Make uncertainty visible
“Not supplied” is an answer worth noticing. Save a dated copy of the terms and ask the provider about unclear wording before proceeding. Avoid treating a comparison badge as a guarantee.

## Compare in one unit
Use one currency, one time period and one treatment of costs. A headline annual percentage does not tell you whether the product meets your goal. The [savings guide](/articles/compare-savings-without-chasing-headline-rates) walks through the comparison method.
